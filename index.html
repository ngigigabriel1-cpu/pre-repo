The Man Behind the Practice

Joseph Nginya Kiiru is a distinguished Consulting Counseling Psychologist and Behavior Change Consultant with a deep specialization in organizational psychology and interpersonal relations. With an academic foundation including two Master’s Degrees—one in Clinical Psychology and another in Psychology and Counseling—Joseph bridges the gap between scientific theory and practical life application.

Beyond his private practice, Joseph serves as a respected lecturer in the Faculty of Psychology and Counseling at Daystar University and Africa Nazarene University. As the founder of Eden Consultants, he has dedicated his career to empowering individuals and 21st-century organizations through transformative mentorship, team building, and life skills training.

Joseph’s commitment to healthy relationships is mirrored in his personal life. He is a devoted family man, married to Esther, and a proud father to his three daughters: Christabel, Christine, and Cindy. He believes that strong individuals build strong families, which in turn build thriving organizations.
