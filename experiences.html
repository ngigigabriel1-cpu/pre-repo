Expert Facilitators & Consultants

Our team comprises certified psychologists, counselors, trainers, and organizational consultants with diverse backgrounds and deep expertise. Each team member is vetted for competence and holds relevant professional credentials.

Joseph Nginya Kiiru - Founder & Lead Consultant

Joseph is a Consulting Psychologist and Behavior Change Consultant with specialized expertise in organizational psychology. He holds a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology and Master's degrees in both Clinical Psychology and Psychology & Counseling. Joseph is a certified TOT (Trainer of Trainers) and serves as a part-time lecturer at Daystar University and Africa Nazarene University's Psychology faculties. With experience consulting for international organizations including UNHCR, WHO, and CDC, Joseph brings clinical rigor and proven facilitation excellence.
