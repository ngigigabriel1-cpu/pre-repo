Organizational Success Through People Care

At Eden Consultants, we understand that employee personal and work-related problems directly impact organizational productivity and corporate image. Our corporate programs strengthen employees' ability to cope with challenges, enrich work relationships, and support sustainable organizational performance. We combine assessment, counseling, and practical training to meet your organization's unique needs.

Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

Our Employee Assistance Programs provide comprehensive support to help employees navigate personal and professional challenges. We offer personalized assessment, counseling, psychotherapy, and referral services designed to strengthen employee resilience and organizational wellbeing.

What We Provide:

  • Individual counseling and psychotherapy sessions
  • Psychological assessment and evaluation
  • Referral services to specialized resources
  • Crisis intervention and support
  • Workplace wellness program design
  • Stress management and coping strategies
